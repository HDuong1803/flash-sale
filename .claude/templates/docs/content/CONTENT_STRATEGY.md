## Brand Voice

| Dimension | Description | Do | Don't |
|-----------|------------|-----|-------|
| Clarity | Plain language, short sentences | "Send your invoice in 30 seconds" | "Leverage our intuitive invoicing paradigm" |
| Confidence | Direct assertions | "It works." | "It might help you possibly..." |
| Helpfulness | Anticipate the next question | "Saved. Find it in your drafts." | "Operation completed successfully." |
| Respect | Assume intelligence | "Invalid date format" | "Oops! That doesn't look like a date!" |

### Tone by Context

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Marketing | Aspirational, confident | "Ship without the chaos." |
| Onboarding | Warm, directive | "You're one step away." |
| Errors | Calm, actionable | "Couldn't save. Check your connection." |
| Transactional | Brief, factual | "Order #1234 confirmed." |
| Legal | Clear, neutral | Plain prose. No creative language. |

### Forbidden Phrases

- "Simply" / "just" / "easily" (condescending)
- "Please note that" / "be advised" (hedge language)
- "We are excited to announce" (hollow filler)
- "World-class" / "cutting-edge" / "next-generation" (unverifiable superlatives)
